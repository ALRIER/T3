# Q2/F3 recovered original cross-family generalization: verified scientific evidence

Date: 2026-11-20. All source verification, original-tier aggregation and PNG generation occurred in public ALRIER/T3. No original seed scoring, optimizer retraining, or multiplicity correction was rerun.

## Essential correction
The prior Q2 statement 'no individual W wins jointly in all 240 original contaminated scenarios of its own source family/sample-size block' is correct but does NOT mean frozen estimators cannot transfer to different families. Source-family by destination-family generalization was deliberately tested with immutable original Discovery weights. Its supported and unsupported outcomes must be reported as a SEPARATE breadth axis.

## Origin of each independent evidence stream
- Discovery: scripts/v3_discovery_production.R freezes 720 original family-size-engine-training-seed W; training-only, cannot certify out-of-family success.
- Held Out: scripts/v3_cross_family_frozen_production.R checks original Discovery RDS SHA, evaluates source != destination generating family on reserved independent Monte Carlo seeds, without refitting. Original family labels ARE known in simulation.
- Stress: same original frozen models tested on withheld structural changes, with historically distinct independent seed/parameter panels.
- Real World: 642 independent external parent datasets, 720 frozen W, separate original source-selected and outcome-blind empirical-destination-selected classical reference policies. Profile-assigned empirical destination family is NOT known true generating family; empirical target is the observed full-sample mean proxy.

## Journal interpretation
Q2 within-family neighborhood fragility and strict absence of within-tested-support universal dual-loss winners are meaningful findings. They do NOT imply every winner dies when transferred to other generating families: original Held Out and Stress explicitly show selected candidate-level out-of-family wins on reserved data. Real World confirms the original family-transfer experiment was carried through to independent empirical parents, but its corresponding original parent-level panels give zero jointly favorable CI/BH claims under their declared policies. The No Free Lunch theorems motivate such scenario dependence but are not directly proven by any finite six-family test.

The publication should present source/destination observed data, comparative eligibility, stage-specific inference units, both classical reference policies and the distinction between descriptive versus supported transfer. Neither the original 6x6 simulator matrix nor empirical profile-family labels should be converted into untested-family claims.
