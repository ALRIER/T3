# Stage 12A — Original-only calibration feasibility audit

The 216 original method comparisons have 10 independent evaluation seeds in 18 family/sample-size blocks, and the source reports 500 MC draws for conditional Q95. These are valid sources for existing point estimates and sensitivity checks. **They do not generate independent known-truth replays of the complete selection-and-inference pipeline.** The T2 null diagnostic has a different experimental scope and is not substituted for RQ1 calibration.

No empirical nominal coverage, full-pipeline FWER, or FDR may be claimed from this audit. Stage 12B remains conditional and must be separately implemented and completed before making those claims.
