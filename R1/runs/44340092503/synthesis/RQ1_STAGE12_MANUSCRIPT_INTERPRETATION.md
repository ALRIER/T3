# RQ1 Stage 12 — Known-truth calibration and inferential limits

The frozen originally scored 10×10 method grids for 18 population/sample-size blocks were used to construct a centered empirical crossed-effects surrogate. For each of three known-truth regimes, 4,096 independent synthetic outer replays were generated per block. We evaluated crossed 9-df 95% interval coverage and global Holm/BH error rates for 216 contrasts. Original predeclared results were not modified.

The global-null and mixed-null regimes provide **surrogate-conditional**, not distribution-free, calibration estimates. A separate frozen-method selection diagnostic compares independent training-only candidate choice with same-evaluation-sample choice. Neither reproduces the original nested HPO or full weight-selection pathway. The manuscript must not claim full selection-aware coverage, exact small-sample type-I control, or empirical full-pipeline FWER from this experiment.

Numeric tables, 648 per-contrast coverage rows, and SVG/PNG figures are in this folder. No original optimization or Monte Carlo simulations were rerun.
