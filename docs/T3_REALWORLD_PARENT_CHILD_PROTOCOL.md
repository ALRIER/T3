# T3 public-data profile audit: parent/child protocol

This module reuses the workflow pattern of the earlier external audit—build a
candidate catalog, profile it without looking at model scores, freeze eligible
sources, score frozen models, and aggregate uncertainty at the independent
source level. It does not copy the earlier dataset sample or treat its family
labels, thresholds, or conclusions as T3 evidence.

## New sample and hierarchy

The candidate inventory must contain at least 642 distinct parent sources. The
frozen audit sample is 642 parents, selected before scoring by a fixed seed.
At least 200 parents must be in the finance stratum; the other 442 are sampled
from non-financial sources. If the verified inventory cannot meet both counts,
the freeze stops instead of counting multiple columns or transforms as extra
independent datasets.

`parent_dataset_id` identifies the independent source object. A parent may
contain several child series or numeric variables (`dataset_id`). Child rows
may receive different empirical profiles, but every inferential and bootstrap
resample groups by the parent ID. A child, transformed series, depth slice, or
bootstrap draw never increases the independent parent count.

## Outcome-blind matching to T3

The input profile file contains raw-vector paths and hashes, sample size,
skewness, excess kurtosis, support, a robust 3-MAD tail fraction, source domain,
and an explicit source sector. The selector first checks the 321-row T3 grid
and source hashes. It then maps profiles to T3 training families and assigns
each parent-child evaluation a nearest supported contamination/MAD cell. This
cell and the proximity scope are frozen before loading any W* scores.

Scopes are retained as parallel reporting strata: `within_regime`,
`within_family_neighborhood`, and `cross_family_transfer`. Every selected
parent is crossed against the six source-family W registries, so the
nonmatching strata remain visible as transfer results rather than disappearing
from the analysis. The matched scenario/mechanism fields are empirical
proxies; they do not claim that the observed source was generated by a known
synthetic contamination mechanism.

## Scoring and inference

T3 scoring evaluates only frozen Discovery weights. The reference is the
sample-mean component for every family and profile. MSE and q95 gains are
reported separately and jointly; Pareto-only and joint wins remain distinct.
The real-world estimand is agreement with each parent’s full-sample mean proxy,
not recovery of an independently known population expectation.

All child variables, depths, sample sizes, and repeated scoring conditions are
paired first and collapsed within `parent_dataset_id`. Confidence intervals
resample parent datasets, not rows or bootstrap children. One global
Benjamini–Hochberg family covers the reported source-family × proximity-scope ×
model × metric panel. The bootstrap and multiplicity plan follows standard
resampling and FDR references (Efron & Tibshirani, 1993; Benjamini & Hochberg,
1995); the simulation and estimand distinctions follow Morris et al. (2019).

## Current execution gates

The selector is fail-closed unless the profile inventory contains 642
independent parents, including 200 finance parents and 442 other parents. The
separate publication/redistribution gate still requires a verified license
record for every source; a candidate catalog or a public URL alone is not
license verification. Discovery artifacts must also exist before the scoring
workflow can run.
